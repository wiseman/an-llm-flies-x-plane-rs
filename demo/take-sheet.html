Freeform take: you're Van Nuys Tower

Skyhawk 2SP, a Cessna 172, starts on a right downwind for 16R at KVNY. You work it live over push-to-talk; OBS records the whole thing.

1 · Set up

  1. Headset in, then make it the Mac's mic and speakers: swift scripts/demo/audio_device.swift input HyperX swift scripts/demo/audio_device.swift output HyperX
  2. Start X-Plane 12, then size its window to the left half (16:9): swift scripts/demo/xplane_window.swift 0 30 2456 1410
  3. Open the take terminal and drag it to the right half: open -na Ghostty.app --args --window-save-state=never --title="sim-pilot take" --font-size=20 --working-directory=$HOME/src/an-llm-flies-x-plane-plan
  4. Start OBS, then build the scenes and audio (run it again after restarting X-Plane or the terminal): uv run scripts/demo/obs_setup.py --terminal "sim-pilot take" Scenes: Cockpit + Terminal, Terminal + Cockpit, Cockpit, Terminal. Your mic is on track 2, desktop audio on track 3.

2 · Run

In the take terminal:

scripts/demo/take.sh demo/kvny-16r-freeform.yaml

It builds, loads the 172 on the downwind, and settles for about 6 s. Then it rolls by itself: OBS starts recording, the terminal opens on the spatial view, and the pilot calls you.

3 · Controls

Tab (hold)Talk as ATC. Release, check the transcript, then Enter to send. Fix "16 right/left" if it's misheard.
Space (hold)Talk to the pilot off the radio: "how far are we from Burbank?"
Ctrl-VSpatial view ↔ log. After each plan the code shows for 12 s, then it goes back. Press Ctrl-V yourself and it stays where you put it.
OBSSwitch scenes yourself, from OBS or the phone remote.

4 · Stop

  • Esc (or Ctrl-C) in the TUI. That ends the take and stops the OBS recording.
  • Don't close the terminal window or kill -9: the recording would keep going.
  • The video goes to ~/Movies, the take's log and timeline to output/sim_pilot-<time>.*.
First take: afterwards, have Claude measure your mic against X-Plane on the recording, and set the gain.

5 · Script

Wait for each readback before the next call. Say "Two Sierra Papa" and "one six right/left".

A · The fixes

Tower 119.3
After its first call:"Skyhawk Two Sierra Papa, Van Nuys Tower, extend downwind, I'll call your base."
Past the 16R threshold:"Two Sierra Papa, make a right three-sixty for spacing, then continue downwind."
After the 360:"Two Sierra Papa, change to runway one six left, continue downwind."It should keep waiting for your base call.
20–30 s later:"Two Sierra Papa, turn base, runway one six left, cleared to land. Keep it at seventy knots on final, traffic behind you."
Slowing after touchdown:"Two Sierra Papa, turn right at Golf, contact Ground one two one point seven."
Ground 121.7
After its call to Ground:"Skyhawk Two Sierra Papa, Van Nuys Ground, taxi to Prop Park via Golf, Alpha, cross runway one six right."Give the crossing in this call: it can't hold short on the way to parking.

B · Landmarks

Tower 119.3
After its first call:"Skyhawk Two Sierra Papa, Van Nuys Tower, extend downwind, remain south of the 118, I'll call your base."
About abeam the numbers:"Two Sierra Papa, traffic is a jet on a four-mile final. Proceed direct Cal State Northridge and hold over the campus, I'll call you back."
After a lap or two:"Two Sierra Papa, traffic no factor. Leave the campus, enter a right base runway one six right, cleared to land."
Then:"…turn right at Golf, contact Ground one two one point seven." → Ground: "…taxi to Prop Park via Golf, Alpha."No crossing needed from 16R.

Extras to improvise

  • "Turn base at Nordhoff" / "extend your downwind to Devonshire, turn base there"
  • "Remain east of Reseda Boulevard"
  • "Follow the 405 southbound, keep it off your left wing"

Don't call base while it's still beside the runway: it turns right away and the final ends up too short.
